- Everything (slides, video, demo, pictures) in **one .pptx**. **Deadline Sat 09:30, no exceptions**, via the submission form.
- Pre-recorded demo highly recommended (sound or silent). Timer visible; sound/clap at 1 min left and at 0:00.
- Q&A: be concise, don't repeat the pitch.
- Few slides, little text per slide, no small text or tiny diagrams, don't switch slides too fast.

## Criteria (use as the structure guide)
- **Impact:** does it wake finance decision-makers up: now is the time to act?
- **Business model:** attractive to customers/users? Compelling business case?
- **Progress:** how much was done during the event?
- **Innovativeness:** how does it differ from classical solutions on the market? More effective or efficient?
- **Methodology:** demo or simulation shown? Quantum used, in practice or theory?

## Story structure (from the workshop)
1. **Hook:** a bold statement or striking question.
2. **Problem:** one sentence, clearly fits the challenge.
3. **Solution:** what we offer, how it works, **why customers would use it** (value).
4. **Innovative:** how it differs from existing solutions; show in a few words that we did our research.
5. **Business model and plan:** use case, maturity, how to take it forward.
6. **Hackathon journey:** technical progress before and during the event; resources and tools (not too detailed).
7. **Demo:** what the end product is right now (pre-recorded).
